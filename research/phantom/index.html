How the research changed

Failures shaped the method.

Reject shortcuts

Generated and observed activity differed before coordination was considered. Neutral and origin probes invalidated attractive early scores.

Preserve the comparison

Matched worlds hold declared actor marginals fixed. Collision checks caught a duplicate mismatch that would otherwise contaminate relational evidence.

Separate ranking from alerts

A raw score ranked treatments perfectly while alerting on 22 of 180 controls. Local permutation normalization was evaluated to address source-specific baselines.

Keep the hard negatives

Popularity and release shocks alerted in all 12 moderate worlds per family. Shifted overlap reduced reconstruction F1 to 0.3636. A broad operating claim failed.

Change the output contract

PHANTOM preserves evidence, missing provenance and equally supported alternatives. A structural alert does not become a finding of intent.

Read the protocol and verification history

A namespace specification was made executable and resealed before final histories were accessed. The first execution lock was superseded after adapters were completed, before event-column access or population selection. The canonical scientific evaluation ran once; planned deterministic replay covered all 756 worlds.

The original verifier failed while serializing its receipt. That failure remains preserved. A separate post-final forensic verifier confirmed the immutable outputs and all 18 scientific gates without rerunning selection, construction, the detector or null generation. Local chronology is not independent institutional verification.

What did not hold up

Behavioral vetoes erased useful structure. Extra non-veto signals added no ranking gain in the tested comparisons. Common stimuli and shifted overlap defeated broader robustness requirements.

Those failures remain part of the research result. They motivate more careful interpretation, not retroactive claims that failed gates passed.

The information limit

Two causes.
The same visible events.

Intentional coordination

An organizer asks a group to align its listening.

A shared external stimulus

A recurring broadcast prompts the same listening.

→

Identical users, items and times

If all permitted observations are identical, an event-only method receives no information that separates the two accounts. Randomness independent of the cause cannot add it.

This is a concrete application of established observational-equivalence reasoning, not a claim that every real case is ambiguous. Exposure, referral or organizer provenance could change the investigation.

From evidence to investigation

Uncertainty is part of the interface.

The read-only dashboard keeps support status, constituent evidence, causal alternatives and missing provenance together. Complete ambiguity sets retain equal scientific priority through filtering and export.
