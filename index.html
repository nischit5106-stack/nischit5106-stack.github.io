The initiative

Better systems start
with better questions.

A model can score well and still miss the point. NEXUS explores the gap between building a system that works in a test and understanding what its evidence supports.

How NEXUS approaches research

Build with curiosity.
Test with skepticism.

01 / BUILD

Make the question tangible.

Turn a real problem into a system that can be examined and tested.

02 / TEST

Look for what breaks.

Challenge the data, the comparison and the assumptions behind a good score.

03 / PRESERVE

Keep the failures.

Document results that narrow the claim, even when they complicate the story.

04 / INTERPRET

Separate evidence from inference.

Say what the observations support and leave the unanswered questions visible.
